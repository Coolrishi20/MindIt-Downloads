**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 189** · 19 November 2026

## Players' phones
- **Your team's name in full.** The card at the top of every phone now gives the team name its own line, up to two lines and never cut off. Before, a long name showed as "The Magni…", and once the question timer appeared it shrank to "The …". The place ("1st of 4"), the timer and the score now sit on a second line underneath.
- **The wager screen fits the phone.** On the Mastermind It! and Blind It! wager screens, "Lock In" used to sit below the bottom edge, so teams had to scroll down while the clock ran. Now everything fits:
  - one short line under the title instead of four;
  - a shorter amount box and keys that are still easy to tap;
  - "Lock In" pinned to the bottom, so it can't scroll away.
  - On smaller phones the score leaves the top card while the team wagers. It's already shown in "Bet Đ0 to Đ…" just below.

## Galaxy Tab held upright
- **Two host cards per view.** The cards under the question (Timer, Scoring, Teams & Turn Order, Host Cam and so on) slide sideways in a strip. Before, the strip showed one card at a time, so Scoring and "A Different Team Answered?" sat off the right edge. Now two cards show side by side, starting with Timer and Scoring.
- **The other cards are one swipe away.** Page dots and a "Swipe for …" line under the strip name them; tap the line to move along.

## Mastermind It! wagers: All in and ½ Half for the host
- Each team's row in the wager panel now has **All in** and **½ Half**. One tap enters and saves that team's wager. This works on the laptop, on the Galaxy Tab hosting the game, and on the Galaxy Tab as the laptop's remote. On the Galaxy Tab held upright they sit beside the amount box, so all the teams and Close wagers still fit on one screen.
- **All in** is the team's maximum. **½ Half** is half of it, rounded down to a whole dirham and never below the minimum, the same as Online Mode's buttons.
- If a team already has a wager (from its phone, a sealed code, or typed by you), the app asks before replacing it.
- A team that can only wager Đ0 gets no buttons. Online Mode keeps its own All in · Half · 0 buttons, and the Blind It! wager is unchanged.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
