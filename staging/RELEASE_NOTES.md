**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 186** · 15 November 2026

## Delete a quiz you haven't played
- A quiz made in **▶ Prepare tonight's quiz** but not begun can now be thrown away: **🗑 Delete this quiz** on the **Check** page, and **🗑 Delete quiz** in Setup's *Prepare tonight's quiz* bar.
- It asks first. Its name, teams, rounds and questions are removed and Setup starts again from the defaults. Your players, Question Database and Historic Games stay, and a copy goes to **🛟 Safety Backups** (Setup → Advanced → Backups & Restore) in case you change your mind.
- The button only shows while nothing has been played. It is not there during a game or while one is paused.

## The Now Playing card, fixed
- With 5, 6 or 7 teams, the **"Now Playing · 1st"** label on the team that's playing had shrunk to a small box stuck to the top of the card, so the position looked cut off. It is now the full-width band it is with 2–4 teams, on the TV, the laptop and the Galaxy Tab, in every theme. On the TV a long team name now stays inside the card.
- With 8 or more teams (the scores down the side) nothing changes.

## The Rascala! badge on the team cards
- The cowboy cat on the last-place team's card was checked on the TV, the laptop and the Galaxy Tab with 2 to 10 teams. Two places were wrong and are fixed:
  - **With 9 or more teams** (scores down the side) the cat covered the team's own picture. It now sits beside the place pill, as it already did with 8 teams, a little lower and smaller so a long team name never runs into it.
  - **When the last-place team is also the team playing**, the cat sat half on the end of the "Now Playing" band. It now sits neatly inside the band, at its left end.
- Which team gets the cat is unchanged.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
