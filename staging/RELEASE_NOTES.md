**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 170** · 3 November 2026

The Galaxy Tab can now save the night if the laptop stops, the TV has a lobby before the quiz, and every team takes its night home as a picture. Also in this release: the Mac app and the refreshed guides.

## 🛟 The Galaxy Tab as backup host
While the **Galaxy Tab app** is the laptop's remote, it keeps a copy of the quiz: the scores, the board, the question on screen, the timer and the sealed Mastermind It! wagers. The laptop's top bar says **🛟 Backup: Galaxy Tab · up to date**, and the tablet says **🛟 Backup ready**.

- **If the laptop stops answering** (a crash, a power cut, or it left the Wi-Fi), after about 10 seconds the tablet offers **🛟 Take over on this tablet**. One tap and the quiz carries on there, on the same question. The timer waits until you resume it.
- **Team phones and TVs follow by themselves:** the Mind It! TV, a TV's own browser and the teams' phones, with no codes to type. A TV on the laptop's HDMI cable goes dark with the laptop: move the cable to the tablet, or use the Mind It! TV.
- **The laptop never scores that quiz again.** If it comes back, it says the Galaxy Tab took over and stops. Opened again later, it won't offer to resume that quiz.
- Take over only if the laptop is really off, frozen or off the Wi-Fi. If it's just slow, press **Keep waiting for the laptop**. Anything in the last second before it stopped can be lost.
- Both the laptop and the Galaxy Tab need build 170. The Mind It! TV app on the Mi Box needs no update.

## 🚪 TV lobby
Before you press **Begin game**, the TV shows your **welcome message**, a **countdown to the start time**, and each team's name as its phone joins ("3 of 4 teams have joined"), with the join QR code below. Set it in **Setup → Audience View & Presentation**: the message, and an optional start time. With no start time it says "The quiz starts soon".

## 📲 Every team's night as a picture
At the end, each team's phone shows its night as a picture sized for phone stories: its place, score, how far from 1st, and its best moment of the night. **Save picture** keeps it. Most phones don't allow their Share button on a page from your Wi-Fi, so press and hold the picture to share it on WhatsApp or Instagram.

## Also in this release
- **The Mac app** (Apple Silicon), every 5th build.
- **The guides,** updated for builds 169 and 170: the Complete Idiot's Guide, the Quick Start, the Quizmaster Cheat Sheet and the Players' Guide.
- **Fixed:** if the laptop app stopped suddenly (a crash or a power cut) soon after you pressed Begin game, it could open again on Setup and not offer the quiz for Resume. It now finds the quiz in its complete copy on the laptop and offers it.
- No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed. Old saves and backups open as before.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
