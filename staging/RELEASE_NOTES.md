**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 165** · 1 November 2026

**⬆ Update App** on the main menu, and every screen's layout checked again on every device.

## ⬆ Update App on the main menu
- **Laptop app:** a new Update App tile beside Rehearsal and Settings. It shows your build and whether a newer one is ready, downloading or downloaded (with a gold edge when there is one). Tap it for the same updater as Setup → Advanced → App Update: download, check, then restart into the new build. Restarting waits until the game is over.
- **Galaxy Tab:** an Update App tile on the home screen. Tap it to check the download page. When a newer build is there, tap again to download and install it. A newer build on the laptop you're connected to is offered too.
- **The web page** says it always opens the newest build from the web link, and offers a reload.

## Every screen checked again
- **Where:** the TV (Audience View) at 9 screen sizes and in all 14 themes, the laptop app, the Galaxy Tab (landscape and portrait), a phone used as the host, the tablet remote, the phones, the TV page and the Galaxy Tab's home screen.
- **What:** every menu item and panel, opened two or three levels deep.

These didn't fit, and are fixed:
- **Returning champions with 6 teams:** the 👑 Champion badge made the score cards taller, and the board's category names were squeezed until they were cut off. The badge now shows the crown and the count (👑 ×2) when 5 or more teams sit in a row.
- **The Gaming theme with 8 or more teams:** the board's last column was hidden under the score column, and a long score ran into its "1st" badge.
- **The Comic theme with 5–6 teams:** the category names and the bottom row of the board were cut off.
- **Galaxy Tab, upright, during a game:** in the cards you swipe sideways, "Now Playing", the team and "Next question" ran together on one line, and the team rows lost their layout.
- **Galaxy Tab home screen, upright, with no games saved yet:** big gaps between the logo, the text and the tiles. It now sits together in the middle.
- **Laptop main menu in a short window** (a small screen with Continue Last Game showing): it no longer runs past the bottom.
- **A phone used as the host:** player rows with a "Currently in …" note overlapped the next row, and long category words were cut in "Pick a Clue".
