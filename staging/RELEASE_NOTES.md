**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 174** · 5 November 2026

A premium Diwali theme, and a clearer name for the Setup section with the theme and TV settings.

## 🪔 Diwali, premium
Choose **Diwali** in Setup → Presentation → Theme.

- **The sky:** a deep aubergine night with a warm glow from below. Fine gold dust replaces the grey sparkles, and a large gold rangoli mandala turns slowly behind everything.
- **The scenery:** warm fairy lights under the marigold toran and a palace skyline in silhouette, with domes and lit jharokha windows. Brass diyas with soft flames line the bottom, and a few marigold petals drift down. The fireworks and sky lanterns stay.
- **The board:** ruby tiles with a gold edge, a small lit diya on every square already played, and plum category plaques with a gold rule.
- **The question card:** a gold frame with lotus corners and a faint mandala; the value in gold foil; the text as readable as before.
- **The score cards:** gold rims and gold "Now playing" pills. The team in play keeps its white spotlight, so it is never mistaken for the orange Streak Flames.
- Everything sits where it always did; only colours, frames and scenery change. With Theme Intensity set to Subtle, or with reduced motion, nothing moves.

## ⚙️ "Audio" is now "Presentation"
The fifth Setup section holds the theme, sound, board & flow, motion, Audience View and Host Cam settings, so it is now called **Presentation**: on its button, in the tab row and on the Next / Back buttons. Everything inside is where it was.

## Also in this release
- **Setup's theme gallery:** with Diwali chosen, the other themes' small previews used to pick up Diwali's decorations. Each now shows its own scenery.
- No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed. Old saves and backups open as before.
- The guides are build 171's. They still call the section "Audio & Presentation"; the next guide refresh comes with build 175.
- The Mac app stays at build 170. The next Mac app is due with build 175.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
