**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 193** · 23 November 2026

In Online Mode, players can now see the scores while they decide their Mastermind It! wager.

## Scores on the Final Round wager screen (Online Mode)
On a Teams, Zoom or Meet call, the players watch the Audience View you share. Until now, the Mastermind It! wager screen showed only each team's name and "? ? ?", so nobody could see how much they had to bet. Now each team's card shows:
- its place (1st, 2nd …) and its score, with the cards in score order;
- what it can bet, for example "Can bet 0 – 3,400".

A team below 0 still has its card, with its score in red and "Below 0 · can't bet". Teams on the same score share a place.

The wagers stay secret: the cards keep showing "? ? ?" until you reveal them, as before. The teams still send their wager to you in a private chat message, and you type it in on the laptop as before.

Only Online Mode's Final Round wager screen changes. Classic and Full Show, the special-question wager screens and your laptop's own screen are as they were.
