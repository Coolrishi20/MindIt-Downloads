**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 168** · 2 November 2026

Rewind It! gets tougher on 4× and 5×, and gets a twist: the **🎡 Wild Rewind It!**

## 🤠 Rewind It!: 4× and 5× lose 3.5×
A wrong answer on a Rewind It! square loses the spin's value, as before — except on **4× and 5×, which now lose 3.5×** (they lost 3×). Right answers still win the value × the spin. The TV, your buttons, the team's phone and the Night Recap all use the new numbers.

## 🎡 Wild Rewind It!
With **3 or more question rounds** (Mastermind It! doesn't count), the **last question round** — the one before Mastermind It! — hides **one extra square: the Wild Rewind It!** Its usual Rewind It! squares keep the normal wheel. The TV says **WILD REWIND IT!** and spins the **Wild wheel**: five slices, one chance in five each.
- **🛡️ Safety Net** — 2×. A wrong answer costs nothing.
- **🃏 Wildcard** — the team chooses its multiplier, 1× to 5×, before seeing the question. You tap their choice (or press 1–5).
- **🏹 Robin Hood** — right wins 2× and the last-place team gets 1× as a gift (one Undo takes back both). Wrong loses 1×. If the team that spun is in last place itself: right wins 3×, no gift.
- **😼 Rascala's Choice** — the last-place team chooses the multiplier (1× to 5×) for the team that spun. You tap their choice.
- **🥔 Hot Potato** — 3×. The team answers it, or passes it once to any team, who has to answer: right wins 3×, wrong costs them nothing. Kept and wrong: −3×.

It's on by itself with 3+ question rounds. To play without it: **Setup → Rounds → Special Question → 🤠 Classic wheel only**, or **Wild Rewind It! → Off** in Prepare tonight's quiz. Same as last time and Rematch keep your choice. In Rehearsal, the bots make their own choices.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
