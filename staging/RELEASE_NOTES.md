**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 184** · 13 November 2026

## Player and team photos fixed in every theme
- With any theme other than **Default Mind It!**, every player photo in Player Profiles and its leaderboard, and every team photo in the scores list beside the board, on Setup's team cards and in the Connect Device list, showed only a small corner of the picture, blown up. The whole photo now fills its circle, centred, in every theme. Your photos themselves were never changed: only the way they were drawn.
- The Galaxy Tab remote's team photo is now centred as well (it showed the top-left part of a photo that wasn't square).

## Also in this release
- Galaxy Tab and Mind It! TV apps are build 184; the in-app update works as in build 177.
- The Mac app and the guides are build 180's (unchanged). The next build with a new Mac app and guides is 185.
- Not tried on real devices: checked on the build machine in all 14 themes. The 15-Minute Device Check (in the QC report) is for your own devices.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
