**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 173** · 5 November 2026

Four layout fixes: wagers on the upright Galaxy Tab, the Night Recap on an upright TV, the laptop board's category names, and a small jump in the laptop's right-hand column.

## 📱 Wagers on the upright Galaxy Tab with the TV preview shown
With **📺 Show TV preview** switched on, the Blind It! and Mastermind It! wager screens used to put the preview at the very end of the page. With 12 teams that was nearly two screens down.

- The preview now stays at the **top**, as it does on every other screen.
- With **9 or more teams**, each team is one row: its name, its status and a **✏️ Type** button. Tap it to open that team's sealed-code and amount fields; the button then reads **Done**. One team is open at a time, and it stays open while wagers come in from the phones.
- Typing a sealed code or an amount and tapping Save work exactly as before.
- With the preview hidden (the default), sideways, on the laptop and on phones, nothing changes.

## 📺 The Night Recap on an upright TV
With 9–12 teams on a TV standing upright, the recap's rows were sized for a wide TV: 13-pixel text with half the screen empty. They now use **28-pixel text**, with the stats and highlights still below. Wide TVs and up to 8 teams are unchanged.

## 💻 The laptop board's category names
With the Audience window open, the board's category names are **14 pixels** (were 11). Long names wrap onto a second line; none is cut off.

## 💻 No more 14-pixel jump on the laptop
With the Audience window open, the CORRECT / WRONG / PASS card dropped 14 pixels whenever a phone or tablet sent the laptop a message. It now stays where it is. A real connection request still shows in that place.

## Also in this release
- No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed. Old saves and backups open as before.
- The guides are build 171's; nothing in them changed.
- The Mac app stays at build 170. The next Mac app is due with build 175.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
