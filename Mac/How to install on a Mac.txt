Mind It! for Mac (v1.69, build 125) - Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4), macOS 11 or newer

1. Check: Apple menu > About This Mac should say "Chip: Apple M..." (Intel Macs are not supported).
2. Download "Mind It! for Mac (Apple Silicon).zip" from this folder (one file, about 100 MB).
3. Double-click the zip in your Downloads folder. It unzips to "Mind It!.app".
4. Drag "Mind It!.app" into Applications (replace the old one if you have an older build; your data stays).
5. First open: macOS says it can't check the app. Click Done, then
   Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Mind It! was blocked..." > Open Anyway.
   (macOS 14 or older: Control-click the app > Open > Open.)
6. Allow "find devices on your local network" and incoming connections, so the tablet and phones can connect.

Your data stays on the Mac. To bring players, history and the Question Database from the Windows laptop,
make a backup file there (Backups & Restore > Back Up Now) and restore it on the Mac.
Not yet tried on a real Mac - test it at home before a quiz night. Full details: Complete Idiot's Guide, section 4.1b.
