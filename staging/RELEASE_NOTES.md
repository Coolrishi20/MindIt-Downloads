**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 182** · 11 November 2026

## Lighter saving on the Galaxy Tab
- On the Galaxy Tab app, once the player file has been saved in full, later saves send only what changed **without the photos** while no photo was added, changed or removed — a fraction of the size (about a tenth with a dozen player photos; less with more photos). The tablet writes far less to its storage.
- The complete player file is kept up to date for everyone: the tablet app rebuilds it whenever the quiz page or the laptop's sync reads the profiles, and when the app is closed or put away. Backups, the laptop's sync and older builds always find everything, photos included.
- A photo added, changed or removed is saved in full straight away, as before. Nothing changes on the laptop or in a browser.

## Also in this release
- The download page's tablet check (the release's own safety check on an Android emulator) now tries once more when the app's page doesn't report after reopening, and keeps the emulator's screen and log, so a stopped release shows why. A real save problem still stops the release.
- Galaxy Tab and Mind It! TV apps are build 182; the in-app update works as in build 177.
- The Mac app and the guides are build 180's (unchanged).
- Not tried on real devices: everything above was checked on the build machine only — the 15-Minute Device Check (in the QC report) is for your own devices.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
