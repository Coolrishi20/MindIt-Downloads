**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 166** · 1 November 2026

**The podium before The Rascala!**, the returning champion crowned correctly, **Rewind It! by default**, a cleaner TV score column, a smoother Players tab on the Galaxy Tab, and the Mac app and guides.

## The end of the night: the podium, then The Rascala!
The top three are now revealed before the last place:
1. final standings (the top three stay secret);
2. tonight's awards;
3. **the podium**: 3rd, 2nd, "And the winner is…";
4. **😼 The Rascala!**;
5. Season standings (Online Mode);
6. 🏁 End Quiz & save.

The Rascala! no longer starts by itself during the podium. It waits for **▶ Next: The Rascala!** and stays until you press the gold button. Night Recap, Save picture and Rematch are on every card from the podium on.

## 👑 The returning champion
In Online Mode, the 🎲 random turn order was drawn after the crown was given, so the crown went to whoever was drawn into the champions' place (sometimes a new player). The crown now stays with the right team, wherever it is drawn, and follows it if you change the order. A game saved part-way in build 165 is put right when it opens. The rule itself is unchanged: last game's winning team is crowned only if all its players are together tonight (a one-player winner: the team that player is on).

A game typed in with **Add Past Game** no longer counts as "last game" because it was entered today. The night it was played counts, here and on the main menu.

## 🤠 Rewind It! is the default
A new quiz starts with **Rewind It!**. **Blind It!** is the other choice, listed second in Setup and in Prepare tonight's quiz. A game you saved part-way keeps the one it was started with. "Same as last time" keeps a night where you chose Blind It!.

## The TV's score column
- **With 9 or more teams**, while every team was level (all on 0 at the start), the cards fell apart: names sat on the scores and the pictures moved to the middle. Fixed, on the TV and on the Galaxy Tab's TV picture.
- **The 👑 CHAMPION and 🔥 streak badges** no longer sit on the team names. With "Now playing", the flames join the tag: "Now playing · 🔥 6".

## Galaxy Tab: the Players tab
- **Smooth scrolling:** the blurred background behind Players & History (and every other panel) was re-drawn on every scroll step. On touch screens, panels now sit on a plain dark backdrop: about 3–4 times faster per frame in our tests.
- **Upright:** panels use the whole screen width. Four players a row instead of three, and "Sync devices" stays on the tab row.
- **The web page:** if Player Profiles opened before your players had loaded, it said "No players yet" until you closed it. It now fills in by itself.

## Mac app and guides
- **Mac app (Apple Silicon)** for build 166.
- **Updated guides:** the Complete Idiot's Guide (with the build-166 main menu), the Quick Start and the Quizmaster Cheat Sheet. They cover the new end-of-night order, Rewind It! by default and ⬆ Update App.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
