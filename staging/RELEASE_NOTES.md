**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 159** · 29 October 2026

The Question Database works again with a big library, the main menu fits your laptop window, the bots got personalities (and you can mark them), and the host controls follow the theme.

## Fixed
- **The Question Database froze for 12–15 seconds** every time it opened, changed tab or opened a category — with your 1,264 questions it looked like it wasn't working, and everything felt slow. The check for duplicate questions compared every question with every other one (about 800,000 pairs, three times per draw). It now only compares questions that share a word and remembers the answer until something changes: the same duplicates are found, and the database opens in a blink.
- **The main menu on the laptop** came out a little taller than the window when ⟳ Sync devices and "Up to Date" were showing, so a scrollbar appeared and the bottom row sat on the dotted line. It now tightens its spacing only when it needs to.

## New
- **Bots you mark yourself.** Start A Rehearsal? asks *Who marks the bots?* — **I mark them** (the bot says its answer out loud and on the TV, then waits for you to press Correct or Wrong Answer, like the players; its Mastermind It! answer waits for your verdict) or **Bots mark themselves** (as in 158). Your choice is remembered.
- **Bot personalities: Sparky (Rookie), Byte (Pro) and Quanta (Genius).** They announce the clue they pick ("Food & Drink for 250, please"), say their answer in character ("Elementary. Paris"), pass in character, react when they're marked ("Recalculating…") and announce their Daily Double wager.
- **The host controls take the theme's colours.** With any theme other than Default, the Galaxy Tab remote, the main menu, Prepare tonight's quiz, Setup (tabs, buttons, text boxes, the Begin game bar), the Question Database and Players & History use the theme's colours instead of navy. Gold highlights stay gold; Default looks as before.
