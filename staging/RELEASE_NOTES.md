**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 149** · 23 October 2026

## New
- **The Rascala! is your cowboy cat** — brown cowboy hat with goggles, black bandit mask, red bandana, sheriff star. It's on the TV reveal, the badge in Historic Games, the game page and Career Achievements.
- **“Enna Rascala....Mind It!”** — the tagline, exactly as written, under the big MIND IT! logo on the TV's loading and intro screens, as The Rascala!'s line, and on the Galaxy Tab home screen.
- **The tie-break always decides The Rascala!** — also when every team finishes level on score: the most wrong answers, then the fewest right answers, then they share it. Games already recorded keep what they recorded.
- **Update the Galaxy Tab app on the tablet itself** — no laptop needed. Settings → **App Update → Check For Updates**; the home screen also checks by itself (at most once a day, when it's online) and shows **Update Available**. **Update** downloads the app from the Mind It! download page; Android installs it over the old one and every game, player and setting stays. (The first time, Android may ask you to allow installs from the browser.)
- **The Mind It! sound pack** — recorded sounds from your trailer's sound library, plus new ones made in the same style: the right-answer ding, the wrong-answer “eh-ehh”, the question sparkle, the lock-in buzz, the pass whoosh, the Blind It! and Mastermind It! impact, the score tally, the time's-up horn, the Triple Gamble dice, the Phoenix rise, Bottoms Up's cheers, the Rewind It! whip-crack and the wheel's bell, The Rascala!'s “wah-wah-waaah”, and a studio cheer under the winner fanfare. A theme with its own sounds keeps them (Theme Sounds On); the TV plays them too.

## Better
- **Blind It! and Mastermind It! wager screens** keep clear of the scenery on Comic Book, Christmas, Movies, Summer and Diwali (the Wager Key ran into a roof or the QUIZ! sign).
- **Rewind It!:** on 4× and 5× the laptop's WRONG ANSWER button now shows the real loss (3×); during the spin the tablet no longer shows ✓ / ✗.
- **Undo** (and Reset question / round) now also takes the answer out of the night's statistics, so an undone wrong answer can't make a team The Rascala!.
- After **End Game** you can choose Blind It! or Rewind It! again for the next game.
- Back from The Rascala!, the winner fanfare doesn't play a second time; the automatic reveal waits while the Champions Card is up.
- The theme scenery (the sleigh, the boat, clouds, bats…) keeps moving smoothly instead of starting again every time the TV redraws.
- Faster screen updates on big histories (the Title Case pass does far less work).
- Laptop app: restoring a large backup from Google Drive gets the full 10 minutes.

## Unchanged
- **Rules, timers, wagers, scores, saved games and connections** (apart from The Rascala!'s tie-break above).
- **The guides** are build 147's (next update at build 150). **Mac:** no new Mac app in this build — the Mac download stays build 147's (next at 150). **Version:** still v1.69.
