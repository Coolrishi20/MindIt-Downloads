**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 158** · 29 October 2026

The menu pass you asked for, a fix for replacing a round from a smaller template, and bots for rehearsals.

## New
- **🤖 Bots in a rehearsal.** Start A Rehearsal? now offers up to 3 bots — **Rookie** (gets about 1 in 3 right, slow), **Pro** (about 6 in 10) or **Genius** (about 85 %, quick, wagers to win). They join your real teams for the rehearsal only. On a bot's turn it picks a clue; on a question it thinks, then **says its answer out loud** (the device's own voice) and shows it on the TV and the host bar, and is marked ✓ / ✗ (Undo works as usual) — or it passes. Bots wager on Daily Doubles and Mastermind It! and write a Final answer. In Phone Buzzer Mode they buzz like a phone, so the first buzz still wins. You still run the show (Next, Reveal, timers). End Rehearsal removes them; nothing is recorded.
- **← Back** in Players & History, the Question Database and Backups: go back to the page you came from instead of opening the menu again. **Close** returns to where you started — the main menu, Setup, or the tablet home ("Close · Home").
- **The Question Database remembers your round:** after you add to a round once, the footer offers **➕ Add to Round N** in one tap ("Other round…" for another). **⇄ Move to another round** lists the rounds as buttons (full rounds greyed out) — no number to type.

## Fixed
- **Replacing a round from a template with fewer categories** left the remaining categories with their old questions. They are now emptied (so you can see what still needs filling), the round's card says so before you choose, and ↩ Undo still brings the old round back.
- **Tiles:** the words sit in the middle of every tile — main menu, Prepare tonight's quiz, the tablet home, Question Database categories.
- **Season tab** redesigned (podium for the top three, one clean list, the season's champion and Grand Rascala at the top), and "Oct–Dec" keeps its capital D.
- **The logo** on the main menu has a dark glow behind it so the background no longer runs into it; the tagline keeps its capitals ("Enna Rascala....Mind It!").
- **Ready to Play** tiles all one shape with one icon style.
