**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 155** · 28 October 2026

Every 5th build: the Mac app (Apple Silicon) and the refreshed guides are included.

## New
- **💡 Hint.** In every mode except Online Mode (Full Show included), when teams play with their phones: the team answering first can take its one hint per game from its own phone, before any wrong answer or pass. Only that phone sees the hint (the first letter and the word lengths); the TV shows 💡 on the team. A right answer earns half the value (half the wager on Blind It!), a wrong one costs the full value, and there's no Speed Bonus. A team the question passes to can't use it.
- **A new end of the night**, the same on the laptop, the Galaxy Tab and phones, one step card at a time with a progress strip and ◀ Back:
  1. **Mark** each team ✓ / ✗ / 0 (✎ fixes a typed answer; "Also accept" adds other right answers; in Online Mode type the chat answers here).
  2. **▶ Reveal all, lowest first** (one team every 2.5 seconds, ⏸ Pause any time; one team at a time is still there).
  3. **Final standings**, with the top three kept secret.
  4. **Tonight's awards** on one page (nothing that gives the top three away).
  5. **The Rascala!**
  6. **The podium**: 3rd, then 2nd, then the winner.
  7. **Season standings** (Online Mode).
  8. **🏁 End Quiz & save**, or **🔁 Rematch**.

  With 3 teams or fewer, the standings step is skipped (the podium shows everyone) and the game is still saved.
- **🔁 Rematch:** the same teams, players and rules with new questions (questions you've never played first), ready to check in Prepare tonight's quiz.
- **Online Mode seasons and the Grand Rascala.** Every Online Mode game counts towards a season (October–December, and so on). Each night a player scores one point for every player they finished ahead of, plus one; tied players share the place's points. The last player of the season is the **Grand Rascala** (nobody while everyone is level). Player Profiles → 🏆 Season shows any season.
- **8 new achievements**, given to your old games too. The career one is **🗺️ World Tour**.
- **▶ Start the show:** welcome, teams, the categories (with time to read them) and how to play (5 seconds), hands-free; ⏸ Hold and ▶ Resume.
- **💘 Valentine's Day** and **🎲 The Risky Theme** (14 themes).
- **📺 Connect a TV** on the main menu.

## Better
- **The host panel during play:** the answer sits in the "now" card; "4 of 4 teams can still answer" folds to one line (▾ who); Teams, Host Cam and Phones fold away; Reset game moved into the ⋯ menu.
- **The Galaxy Tab as host:** in portrait, the scoring buttons stay at the bottom and the extra cards sit in a swipe row; in landscape, the board and the panel stay side by side. Mastermind It! wagers in two columns. Buttons are finger-sized (44 px), also with 8 or more teams.
- **A phone as host:** stays upright and says "this phone".
- **The tablet remote** explains each end-of-night step on its big button, and why it waits (for example, "Mark every team Correct or Wrong first").
- **Sound clues** play at an even loudness.
- **The phone's place card** and layout fixes L1–L5.
- **The guides:** the Complete Idiot's Guide is now 14 pages, about Online Mode only.
