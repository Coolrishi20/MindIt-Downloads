**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 181** · 11 November 2026

## Every scoring button now matches the score
- **🔥 Phoenix:** while the Phoenix team answers its Phoenix question, CORRECT shows what the Phoenix really pays (double points plus the catch-up, never past the leader) and WRONG ANSWER shows **−0** — a wrong Phoenix answer costs nothing. Before, both showed the plain question value.
- **🍺 Bottoms Up:** WRONG ANSWER now says "… · 🍺 0 if they finish the drink" — the drink decides whether anything is lost.
- **📱 Phone Remote:** CORRECT shows the **⚡ speed bonus** while it can still be earned, as the laptop does.
- **Galaxy Tab as the laptop's remote (upright):** the lines under CORRECT and WRONG ANSWER show the laptop's own amounts (Phoenix, pass-on bonus), not the plain question value.
- Checked: normal questions, pass-on bonus, speed bonus, Triple Gamble, Bottoms Up, Phoenix, Rewind It! 1×–5× and all five Wild Rewind It! results — every button now says what the score does. Scoring itself hasn't changed.

## Faster saving with a big player database
- With many players, photos, questions and past games, each save now takes about a third of the time it did (at Galaxy Tab speed, 50 players with photos and 5,000 questions: about 0.2 s → 0.07 s), so the screen doesn't stall after scoring. The saved file is exactly the same as before — backups and older builds read it as always.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
