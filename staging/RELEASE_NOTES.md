**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 169** · 3 November 2026

Your host screens, smoother: the team's name on the answer buttons, a "Now answering" panel on the upright Galaxy Tab, a board panel that fits a laptop screen, and a tidier Setup.

## ✅ The team's name on CORRECT and WRONG ANSWER
After a wrong answer the question moves to the next team; now the buttons say who you're scoring, on its own line above CORRECT and WRONG ANSWER. It follows the turn, a Rewind It! square's team and a passed 🥔 Hot Potato. On the laptop, the Galaxy Tab both ways, the tablet as a remote and the phone remote. The keys (C, W) and the buttons work as before.

## 📱 Galaxy Tab upright: Now answering
The question screen now opens with the team that's answering in big letters, the question, and every team's score (teams that already tried are dimmed). **Undo & resets** moved down, below the other cards, so the top of the screen is about the question.

## 💻 Laptop: the board panel fits the screen
On a 1366×768 laptop the board's right-hand panel ran below the screen. Now **Filling the board… Skip** and the round's buttons sit right under Now picking, and the scores follow in slimmer rows.

## 🛠️ Setup and smaller things
- **One gold button on Setup: Begin game.** The Prepare tonight's quiz button now reads **Open the steps ›** with a gold outline.
- The "Add questions to the Question Database" card left Setup in the laptop and tablet apps; the Question Database (with Import Template) is on the main menu and the tablet home.
- On the tablet, Setup now says "this tablet" instead of "your laptop".
- Mastermind It! marking: the third button reads **No loss** (wrong, no penalty) instead of "0".
- After a round, **Save backup file** is outlined, so **Start Round** is the one gold button.

No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
