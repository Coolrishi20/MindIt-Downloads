**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 167** · 2 November 2026

Four new things to look back on: **Rewind It! highlights on the Night Recap**, a **👑 Champions** history, a **🏆 Season finale** on the TV, and **⚔️ Head to Head** with each player's nemesis. All of them use **every game in History** — nights you played, games typed in with Add Past Game, and restored backups.

## 🤠 Rewind It! on the Night Recap
In a Rewind It! game, the Night Recap has a new row: **the biggest spin**, **the luckiest spin**, **the unluckiest spin** and **who won most from the wheel** (when one spin says it all: what the wheel gave in all). Each past night's page in **Historic Games** shows the same. Games saved from build 167 on keep each spin's result. Older games kept the spins but not their results, so their page shows the biggest spin, who spun most and the average spin, and says so. The last night played on your laptop or tablet gets its results back from that night's scores.

## 👑 Champions
**Players & History → 👑 Champions**: the current champion and how many nights in a row, the longest reign, the players with most nights wearing the crown, and every reign — who held it, for how many nights, and whether they were **dethroned** (played and lost) or **didn't defend it** (weren't there). It uses the same rule as the crown in a game: the whole winning team (or a one-player winner) must be back together. Each player's page shows "👑 N nights wearing the crown".

## 🏆 Season finale
A ceremony on the TV for a season: "Who takes the trophy?" → **😼 the Grand Rascala** → **3rd** → **2nd** → **🏆 the Season Champion**, with confetti. Start it from **Players & History → 🏆 Season → 📺 Season finale on the TV**, or from **🏆 Season finale** on the Season standings card at the end of an Online Mode night (tonight counts). ▶ Next or Space for each step, ◀ Back, and **💾 Save picture / 📋 Copy picture for the chat**: one picture of the whole season (champion, 2nd and 3rd, the table and the Grand Rascala). ✓ Finish or Esc puts the TV back.

## ⚔️ Head to Head
On each player's page, the Head-to-head tab now starts with their **😈 Nemesis** (the player who has beaten them most — at least two games) and their **🎯 Favourite opponent**. **⚔️ Compare with someone…** (or **Players & History → ⚔️ Head to Head**) compares any two players: wins against each other, nights on the same team, and every night they met. Merged players count as one, everywhere in head to head.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
