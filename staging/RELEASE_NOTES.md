**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 171** · 4 November 2026

When a team's time runs out, the question now passes to the next team by itself, and the laptop's controls stay in one place while the Audience window is open.

## ⏰ Time's up passes by itself
When a team's time runs out, you no longer need to press anything. **Two seconds later** the question **passes to the next team**, and the team that ran out **loses no points**.

- The next team gets **30 seconds**, as after any pass. If it answers right, it earns the usual **pass-on bonus**.
- The TV says **TIME'S UP → TEAM CHAI'S TURN · 30 SEC**. Your screen says why: "Time's up for Desert Foxes — passed to Team Chai, no points lost".
- When the **last team** runs out of time, nobody is left: the question ends as **No one answered**, and no points are lost.
- **Unchanged:** Blind It! and Rewind It! questions and Triple Gamble stay with the team, as before; you mark them right or wrong. Classic answering works as before.
- Did a team answer just as the clock ran out? Press **CORRECT** or **WRONG ANSWER** within those 2 seconds, on the laptop or the Galaxy Tab remote. It scores that team as before, and nothing passes.
- **End Timer** only stops the clock; it doesn't pass.

## 💻 The laptop with the Audience window open
- **Every button is in reach.** On the question and answer screens, the card with **CORRECT / WRONG ANSWER / PASS** now starts at the top of the right-hand column. The timer card and the "Showing in the Audience View Window" note sit on the left. On a 1366×768 laptop, all eight buttons are on screen without scrolling. Before, Pass and Pause timer were cut off.
- **The card stays on the same side** on the question, answer and scored screens. Before, it jumped from left to right.
- **The Undo notice no longer covers a button.** For 8 seconds after a score, it moves to a free spot: the bottom of the screen, or the strip under the top bar.
- **The TV's "Full screen" button hides itself** 10 seconds after the mouse stops, and whenever the window is full screen. Move the mouse to bring it back. The Mind It! TV page already did this.

## 📱 Galaxy Tab
The **Undo** button and Mastermind It!'s **✏️ Change a wager** toggle are now 44 pixels tall, the app's size for fingers.

## Also in this release
- **The guides,** updated for the time-up rule: the Complete Idiot's Guide, the Quick Start, the Quizmaster Cheat Sheet and the Players' Guide.
- No scores, wagers or bonuses changed. Old saves and backups open as before. The Mac app stays at build 170. The next Mac app is due with build 175.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect, which warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
