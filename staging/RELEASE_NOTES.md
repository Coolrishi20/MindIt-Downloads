**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 176** · 8 November 2026

The category names on the TV board are big and readable again, the Galaxy Tab syncs with the laptop by itself when they connect, and a tap on an achievement in Player Profiles shows what it's for.

## 📺 The TV board's category names
On a laptop window or TV picture that is wide but not very tall (for example a full-screen window above the Windows taskbar, or Windows scaling at 125 % or 150 %), the row of category names was squeezed into a thin strip, 9–13 pixels tall, and the names were shrunk until they were hard to read or cut off.

- **Why it happened:** the board split its height between the name row and the five rows of squares, and the squares kept a minimum height for their big money values. When the score cards above took more room (5 or more teams, streak badges), the name row was the only one that could give way, so it shrank almost to nothing.
- **Now:** the name row always keeps room for two lines of text, and the squares give up a little height instead (their values still fit). At 1920×1032 the names are about 27–29 pixels on 86-pixel plaques (they were 14 pixels on 13-pixel strips). They are centred, wrap onto two lines at a word break, and only a name that still doesn't fit is made smaller — on its own, so the other names keep their full size.
- The names fit again by themselves when the window changes size (the Audience window, Present mode, or a laptop moved to the TV).
- The look, colours, theme, score cards, animations and every rule are unchanged.

## 🔄 The Galaxy Tab syncs by itself
When the tablet connects to the laptop's game, the two now sync straight away: players, Historic Games and questions go both ways. While they stay connected, anything new on the laptop (a new player, a finished game) reaches the tablet by itself within a few seconds. If nothing has changed they don't sync again, and a reconnect within 10 minutes only syncs when there is something new. The laptop shows a short note, "⟳ Synced with …", and the ⟳ Sync button is still there.

## 🏅 Achievements in Player Profiles
On the tablet, open **Player Profiles → a player → Achievements** and tap a badge to see what it's for (for example "On Fire: 5 or more correct answers in a row"). Tap it again to close it. Before, the description was only shown when a mouse pointer rested on it, which a tablet doesn't have.

## 🛟 If the tablet's screen stays blank
We couldn't make the tablet go blank when it joins the laptop's game, so we couldn't find a cause to fix. If the tablet's controls haven't appeared after 8 seconds, it now shows a card that says so, with **Try Again**, **Back to the Start** and **Copy Details**. The tablet also keeps a list of its recent errors. If it happens again, tap Copy Details and send us what it copied.

## Also in this release
- No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed. Old saves and backups open as before.
- The Mac app (build 175), the guides (build 175) and the Rehearsal Checklist (build 160) are unchanged.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
