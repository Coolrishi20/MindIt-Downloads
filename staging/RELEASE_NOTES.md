**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 164** · 1 November 2026

Up to **12 teams** (was 10), with every screen checked at 12.

## 12 teams
- Quick Setup's **Number Of Teams** goes to 12, and **+ Add Team** works until 12. The same goes for **🎲 Random Teams**, **Add Past Game**, an Excel team list, saved presets and the rehearsal bots' room.
- A game with 11 or 12 teams saves and loads with the same teams, scores and team codes. Games with 10 teams or fewer are unchanged.
- The laptop app and the Galaxy Tab already kept up to 12 teams for phones, the remote and the TVs, so nothing changed there.

## Every screen checked at 12 teams
Each screen was compared at 12 teams (and at 11) with the same screen at 10:
- every Audience View screen at TV, projector and portrait sizes;
- the laptop and Galaxy Tab host screens, in landscape and portrait;
- the tablet remote;
- two phones on the laptop app;
- Host Cam;
- the standings, recap and champions pictures.

Most screens already fitted. These didn't, and are fixed:
- **The Night Recap picture:** with 11–12 teams its statistics ran into the footer. The rows are now a little shorter. With 10 teams or fewer the picture is exactly as before.
- **Standings mid-game, with 12 long team names:** the last row could run below the TV screen. The rows now take one size smaller, only when they would run over.
- **On an upright (portrait) TV:**
  - the standings under the podium now fit all teams;
  - the Mastermind It! answers keep the question and answer in view, with wager cards six to a row;
  - the standings title no longer covers "Standings · Round N". That last one happened with any number of teams.

## Guides
- The Complete Idiot's Guide and the Quizmaster Cheat Sheet now say 2–12 teams. The other three guides are unchanged from build 160.

## Tests
- The new test checks 11- and 12-team games:
  - saving and reloading;
  - the TV's board, questions and wagers, and every end-of-night screen, at five screen sizes;
  - the recap picture;
  - the upright-TV fixes.
- One older test now follows the team limit instead of assuming 10. Another leaves the new 11 and 12 choices out of its comparison with build 161.
