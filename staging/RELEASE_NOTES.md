**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 185** · 15 November 2026

## Every theme checked on every screen
- Before each release, every game screen (board, questions, answers, wagers, Rewind It!, The Rascala!, Phoenix, Bottoms Up, standings, Mastermind It!, the winner, awards, the Night Recap, tie-breaks and buzzers) and Player Profiles are now checked in **all 14 themes**, on the TV, the laptop and the Galaxy Tab. The check covers two things: every player and team photo fills its circle, and no text is harder to read than with the Default theme.
- **The Risky Theme, fixed:** that first check found text that couldn't be read on the TV in The Risky Theme. The **CORRECT!** and **WRONG ANSWER** banners had pink text on green and red. The buzzer winner's banner was pink on pink, so the name didn't show at all. A team's **Wrong** tag and the returning champion's **👑 Champion** tag were dark on dark. They now use the same text colours as every other theme.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
