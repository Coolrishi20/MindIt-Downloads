**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 148** · 23 October 2026

## New
- **Blind It!** — the Daily Double has a new name and a black-sunglasses logo on the TV. The rules are exactly the same: where the squares go, the wager limits, the scoring, only the picking team answers, no passing. Betting everything is now a **Never Mind It!** moment.
- **Rewind It!** — an optional replacement for Blind It!. Setup → 4 · Rounds & Questions → **Special Question**: Blind It! (default) or Rewind It!, one per game, locked once the game is under way.
  - A Rewind It! square brings up a cowboy-hat wheel (black hat, crimson band). The Quizmaster taps **🤠 Spin The Wheel** (laptop or tablet); it lands on 1×–5×, each equally likely, and the TV shows what's at stake.
  - Right: + value × the number. Wrong: − value × the number, but never more than 3×. With a 500 question: 1× ±500 · 2× ±1,000 · 3× ±1,500 · 4× +2,000 / −1,500 · 5× +2,500 / −1,500.
  - The result is decided once and saved straight away — closing the page, reconnecting or opening the square again never spins again. **Skip The Spin** lands it at once.
  - Only the picking team answers, no passing, no wager. **Every chip (Triple Gamble, Phoenix, Bottoms Up) is off for that game.**
- **Mastermind It!** — the Final Round has a new name, a brain logo and a short intro on the TV when it starts. The rules are unchanged.
- **The Rascala!** — a title for last place, with its own character, **Naughty Pussy** (a ginger tabby in a bandit mask and a tiny top hat).
  - Who: the lowest final score; if level, the most wrong answers; if still level, the fewest right answers; if still level, they share it. If every team finishes on the same score, they're all joint Rascalas. It never changes the standings or the winner.
  - After the winner celebration the TV reveals it by itself (or tap **😼 The Rascala!**): “Rascala! You are now a Naughty Pussy!”. **Continue ▸** goes back. It never holds up saving or End Game.
  - It's an achievement for that team's players (never the Quizmaster), counted in Player Profiles, and shown next to the champion in Historic Games.
  - **Older games count too:** worked out from what they saved, never changed. A tie that the old data can't settle shows **needs review**, and the Quizmaster can pick on that game's page.
- **New sounds** made by the app for Blind It!, the Rewind It! wheel, Mastermind It! and The Rascala!.

## Unchanged
- **Rules, timers, wagers, scores, saved games and connections** (apart from Rewind It!, which only plays when chosen).
- The Quizmaster's **⭐ Go To The Final** button keeps its name.
- **The guides** (Complete Idiot's Guide, Quick Start, cheat sheet, checklist, players' guide) are build 147's; they'll be updated at build 150.
- **Mac:** no new Mac app in this build — the Mac download stays build 147's (Mac apps come every 5th build; next at 150).
- **Versions:** still v1.69.
