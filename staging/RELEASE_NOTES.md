**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 172** · 5 November 2026

The small pop-up notes on the laptop no longer sit on the timer buttons.

## 💬 Pop-up notes stay clear of the buttons
On a laptop with the Audience window open, notes such as "⏰ Time's up for Desert Foxes — passed to Team Chai, no points lost" appear at the top of the left-hand column. Since build 171 that is where the timer card sits. A two-line note used to cover the top of **Pause Timer**, **+30 Sec** and **End Timer** for its 4.5 seconds, just as the next team's clock started.

- Notes now start **under the timer card**, wherever they would otherwise cover a button.
- Nothing moves where nothing is covered: the board, the Galaxy Tab, and the laptop with the Audience window closed look as before.
- Several notes at once still cover no button.

## Also in this release
- No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed. Old saves and backups open as before.
- The guides are build 171's; nothing in them changed.
- The Mac app stays at build 170. The next Mac app is due with build 175.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
