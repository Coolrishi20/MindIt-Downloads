**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 175** · 6 November 2026

Diwali gets its own sounds and a greeting, every theme's preview in Setup shows its own look, and Diwali runs smoothly on older TVs. This is a 5th build: the Mac app and refreshed guides are included.

## 🪔 Diwali sounds
With **Theme Sounds On** and the Diwali theme:

- **A right answer:** a temple-bell chime, two bells ringing out together.
- **A Rewind It! or Blind It! square:** a string of firecrackers, like a ladi: a fuse, one sharp crack, smaller ones after it, and their echo. The Mastermind It! start and a tie-break question, which share this sound, get it too. The Rewind It! wheel's ticks and landing sound stay the same.
- **The winner:** a short shehnai-style flourish over the cheer, on the host's laptop or tablet only.

With Theme Sounds Off, or in any other theme, the sounds are as before. The loading music, the intro, "Enna Rascala....Mind It!" and the explosion never change.

## ✦ Shubh Deepavali ✦
In the Diwali theme the opening title says **✦ Shubh Deepavali ✦** under "Enna Rascala....Mind It!", and the winner screen closes with **✦ Thanks for Playing — Shubh Deepavali! ✦**. Other themes are unchanged.

## 🎨 Setup's theme gallery
Each small preview in **Setup → Presentation → Theme** now shows its own theme's scenery, whichever theme you chose. Before, the chosen theme's decorations covered the others: with Christmas chosen, Halloween lost its moon and Summer its sun.

## 📺 Diwali on older TVs
On the oldest engine the Mind It! TV app supports, build 174's Diwali drew about 41 frames a second; it now draws 59, the same as other themes. The mandala and the gold dust stand still, and two single petals drift down instead of a full layer of petals. The lights, diyas, lanterns and fireworks still move, and the look is the same.

## 📘 Guides and the Mac app
- The Complete Idiot's Guide, the Quick Start, the Quizmaster Cheat Sheet and the Players' Guide are updated for build 175. They name the Setup section **Presentation**, include a "New in builds 172–175" page with the Diwali pictures, and give the new settings paths.
- **Mac app (Apple Silicon)** for build 175. The previous Mac app was build 170.

## Also in this release
- No rules, scores, timers or wagers changed. Old saves and backups open as before.
- The team in play keeps its white spotlight in every theme.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
