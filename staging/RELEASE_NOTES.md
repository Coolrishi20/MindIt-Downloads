## New
- **All 12 themes upgraded to a premium TV game-show standard.** Every theme now has its own look and its own way of handling the big moments: the title when a game starts or ends, each round's category cards, the question reveal (the square still grows into the card), correct and wrong answers, a team taking the lead, the Daily Double, back to the board, the Final Round, the final answer, the final standings and the winner. All 12 share one animation engine, with a "recipe" for each theme on top.
- **Default Mind It!, the flagship:** a midnight-navy studio with royal-blue light and gold, a subtle LED wall behind the board with LED strips that breathe gently, glossy squares with a gold hairline edge, deeper category headers, a layered question card with a gold inner frame, refined team panels, spotlight beams for the game's start, round intros, the Final and the winner, a gold trace round each new question and gold light when a team takes the lead.
- **Comic Book, redesigned as a premium graphic novel:** ink black (#111720), crimson panels (#B92332), hero-gold values (#E9B949), a paper-cream question card (#F2E9D7) and steel-blue light (#315B83). New: ink-panel question reveals, a POW! burst on the team that scores, MISS! on a wrong answer, page-flip category cards and board, a KA-POW Daily Double and a hero-rays winner.

## How it behaves
- Frequent moments are quick (about 1–1.5 seconds) and never hold up the game; the Daily Double, round intros, the Final and the winner are the cinematic ones.
- **Motion Subtle:** a gentle glow only. **Off** or the device's **Reduce Motion:** none of it.
- A slow Wi-Fi TV that lightened itself (build 146) skips the theme moments. The Galaxy Tab hosting a quiz draws half the particles.
- Everything is drawn by the app itself: no new files, no internet needed, no new sounds.
