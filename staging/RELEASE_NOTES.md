**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 161** · 30 October 2026

An older TV or tablet now tells you when it can't show Mind It! properly, the Galaxy Tab is a little quicker in a game, and the long stress test now runs a full hour.

## Older TVs and tablets
The Galaxy Tab and the Mi Box show Mind It! with Android's own web engine ("Android System WebView"). I tried Mind It! in real old versions of that engine:

- **Version 105 and newer** (the version Mind It! TV already asks for): the TV picture, the host screens and the theme colours come out the same as on a new engine. On version 105 the TV page, the tablet remote's buttons and a phone's wager all worked with the laptop app (tried here, not on your devices).
- **Older than 105:** the TV picture comes out the wrong size. Such a device now shows a red message saying which version it has and what to do. The message appears on the TV page, on phones, on the laptop page and on the tablet's home screen. The fix is: open the Play Store and update "Android System WebView" (and "Google Chrome"). "Continue anyway" hides the message.

## Galaxy Tab speed
I tested with a library the size of yours (1,264 questions) on a tablet-sized screen, slowed down to tablet speed.

- Game taps are quicker: starting the game 1.4 s → 0.8 s; marking an answer, or opening a question, about 20% quicker (0.95 s → 0.77 s to mark one right).
- The Question Database's All Questions: 3.3 s → 2.8 s.
- Setup still takes about 1.5 s to redraw on the tablet; it's a long page.
- On the tablet, a category or a search opens faster than All Questions.
- Using the tablet as the laptop's remote was already quick: about a third of a second per tap.

## Tests
The 60-minute soak now plays a full hour: it used to stop after 55 minutes.
