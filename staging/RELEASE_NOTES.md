**Mind It! v1.69 · Build 177** · 9 November 2026

Nine upgrades: ⏰ Time's Up now passes the question like a real time-out, the host sees the 2-second pass and can still score a team that answered just in time, a Sound Check for setting the TV's volume, a slimmer Present-mode toolbar, the tablet's errors reach the laptop, the tablet app recovers if its screen process crashes, and a one-page Quiz Night Run-Sheet.

## ⏰ Time's Up
- The host's **End Timer** button is now **⏰ Time's Up**, and pressing it counts as the clock running out. The clock shows Time's up, the time's-up sound plays and, as when the clock reaches 0, the question passes to the next team after 2 seconds with no points lost. In Classic answering it just ends the clock. Before, End Timer only stopped and reset the clock, and the same team kept the question.
- **The 2 seconds before a time-up pass now show on the host screen:** "⏰ Time's up for Team A — passing to Team B…", with a bar, inside the question card. The tablet remote shows the same line. CORRECT or WRONG ANSWER in those 2 seconds still scores the first team, as before.
- **Answered in time after all?** After a time-up pass, the question card says "⏰ Team A's time ran out — answered in time after all?" with a **Score Team A…** button. It opens **A different team answered?**, where Team A now appears too. Correct takes back the automatic pass and scores Team A (no pass-on bonus for its own pass). Wrong takes the points off, and the team now answering keeps its turn.

## 🔊 Sound Check
**Setup → 5 · Presentation → Sound → 🔊 Sound Check** plays the chosen theme's sounds in turn: a right answer, a wrong answer, a pass, time's up, a Rewind It! square and the winner. It shows what's playing, so you can set the TV's volume before guests arrive. With Diwali and Theme Sounds On you hear the bell, the firecrackers and the shehnai. If the sound is off, it says so.

## 📺 Present mode's toolbar
On the laptop's Present mode, the toolbar that appears when the mouse moves is now one slim line at the very bottom of the screen. Before, it was up to three lines tall and covered 4 to 8 squares of the board's bottom row on a laptop at 100–150 % scaling. Now it covers none at the sizes we tried. If a screen is too short for that, it shows at the top instead.

## 🛟 The tablet
- **Its errors reach the laptop.** When the Galaxy Tab is connected as the Quizmaster remote, it sends the laptop any errors it kept. They appear in **Setup → 6 · Advanced → Diagnostics → Tablet Errors**, with **📋 Copy Error Details**. If the tablet ever goes blank again, copy them from the laptop and send them to us.
- **The app reloads if its screen process crashes.** If Android's web view process stops (it crashed, or Android closed it to free memory), the app now opens the same page again, the laptop's game or the start screen, instead of closing. This needs Android 8 or newer; the Galaxy Tab has it.

## 📋 Quiz Night Run-Sheet
The Rehearsal Checklist is now a **one-page run-sheet for the night itself**: before guests arrive (update, Presentation, Sound Check, TV lobby, the tablet's sync, the backup host, phones), during the quiz (Time's Up, answered in time, Present mode, intermission, a blank tablet) and at the end (finish, sync and back up, the phones' pictures).

## Also in this release
- No other rule, score, timer or wager changed. Old saves and backups open as before.
- The Mac app (build 175) and the guides (build 175) are unchanged.

## Updating the Galaxy Tab
Android may warn that the app could be harmful. That is Google Play Protect: it warns about apps installed from outside the Play Store that can update themselves. Tap the small arrow (**More details**), then **Install anyway**. This build asks for no new permissions.
